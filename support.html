Support

Help for VayuSetu on iPhone, iPad and Mac

Email contact@lokeshreddy.dev and include your device, iOS or macOS version, the VayuSetu version (Settings → About) and what you were doing. Please do not send private keys, passwords or full VPN configuration files.

Turning on Safari adblocking

  1. Open VayuSetu → Adblock and switch on Safari Protection.
  2. On iPhone or iPad open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions. On Mac open Safari → Settings → Extensions.
  3. Turn on each VayuSetu extension (Ads, Privacy, Security, Social, Annoyances, Custom). Extensions with no rules need no switch.
  4. Return to VayuSetu. The Adblock screen shows when Safari has loaded the rules.

A website does not work

Open Adblock → Website Exceptions and add the site. Safari then stops blocking on that site's pages, and VayuSetu DNS stops blocking its name. You can also lower the filtering level from Strict to Balanced or Light.

Connecting a VPN

VayuSetu does not provide a VPN server. Import the WireGuard configuration from your own provider (file, clipboard or QR code) or add an IKEv2 server in Profiles. Then use the power button on Home.

DNS Only mode

DNS Only runs a small local tunnel that sends only DNS questions to the encrypted DNS provider you chose; your other traffic does not pass through VayuSetu.

Data

Everything VayuSetu stores is on your device. Deleting the app removes it. See the privacy policy.
