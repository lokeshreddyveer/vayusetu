# VayuSetu — Setu Stones concept study

Three concepts: Setu Stones (recommended), Devanagari व with an extended beam, and two overlapping glass arcs with a bridge line.

Setu Stones includes 1024px default, dark, light, grayscale-tinted and pure-white transparent artwork. Square canvases have no pre-rounded corners. Editable SVGs and individual full-canvas SVG/PNG layers are included under layers. The composite previews simulate depth; they are not an Icon Composer document or verification of system-rendered Liquid Glass.

At29px, the five-dot arc remains distinguishable, while wind trails and glass-edge details are secondary. Previews at180,120,60 and29px were visually inspected. All variant canvas sizes and opaque/transparent backgrounds were checked. No on-device icon installation or wallpaper-dependent clear appearance test has been performed for this study.

Devanagari alternative: the base letter is the real U+0935 glyph from Kohinoor Devanagari, preserved as an outline. Its extended top beam is a design modification; native-reader review is still appropriate before choosing it as the final identity.

The recommended foreground layers are flat neutral silhouettes. Import them into Icon Composer and apply its materials rather than adding system glass over baked preview highlights. Match supported system appearances and verify contrast against actual wallpaper. Do not equate icon symbolism with independently verified VPN/DNS security.

This is a design comparison package. It does not replace installed icons, rebuild signed artifacts or modify networking code.
