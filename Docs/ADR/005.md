# ADR-005: IPv4 and IPv6 failure policy

Status: Proposed. Date: 2026-10-10.

## Decision

Every policy declares native IPv6 support or enforceable IPv6 blocking. Unsupported NAT64/DNS64 and IPv6-only operation must be visible. Failures cannot produce a verified secure state.

## Acceptance evidence

Test public IPv4/IPv6, DNS paths, denied destinations, IPv6-only networks and transitions. Synthetic capture routes alone are not evidence of leak prevention.
