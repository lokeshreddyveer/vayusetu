# ADR-006: Kill-switch guarantees and exceptions

Status: Proposed. Date: 2026-10-10.

## Decision

Express desired enforcement and known platform exceptions as capabilities. Reject no-exceptions policy combined with direct-DNS bypass. Do not claim an absolute kill switch from includeAllNetworks or route configuration.

## Acceptance evidence

Measure traffic during tunnel loss, resolver loss, path switches and process termination; record system traffic exceptions and product limitations.
