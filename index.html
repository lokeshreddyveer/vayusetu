VayuSetu

Your network. Your control.

VayuSetu is a VPN, encrypted DNS and ad-blocking app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It connects to the WireGuard or IKEv2 server you configure, resolves names through the DNS provider you choose, and blocks ads, trackers and malicious domains using public filter lists that you switch on.

  • VPN — import your own WireGuard profile or add an IKEv2 server.
  • Encrypted DNS — DNS-over-HTTPS or DNS-over-TLS, with optional on-device filtering.
  • Safari adblocking — six content blockers (Ads, Privacy, Security, Social, Annoyances, Custom) that work without a VPN.

VayuSetu has no accounts, no analytics and no servers of its own. Read the privacy policy or get support.
