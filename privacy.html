Privacy Policy

VayuSetu for iPhone, iPad and Mac · Last updated October 14, 2026

Short version: VayuSetu does not collect your data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own. Nothing about your traffic, browsing, DNS queries or configuration is sent to the developer, sold, or shared.

What stays on your device

  • VPN profiles you import or create, including private keys and passwords, are stored in the device Keychain.
  • DNS provider settings (the resolver address you choose) are stored in the Keychain.
  • Filter lists and your own rules are stored in the app's private storage.
  • Connection events and session history (for example "connected" and "disconnected", and byte counters) are kept locally so you can read them in Logs and Traffic. You can clear them in the app.
  • Activity statistics are counts of DNS requests and blocked requests per ten minutes. They contain no website names and stay on the device.
  • Encrypted backups are files you create and export yourself, protected by a password only you know. VayuSetu cannot read or recover them.

Network connections VayuSetu makes

VayuSetu connects only to destinations you configure or ask for:

  • Your VPN server, when you connect. VayuSetu does not operate VPN servers; your traffic goes to the provider you chose, whose privacy policy applies to it.
  • Your DNS provider, over an encrypted connection (DNS-over-HTTPS or DNS-over-TLS), to answer DNS queries. With VayuSetu's local filtering, blocked names are answered on the device and never leave it.
  • Filter list hosts, to download the public blocklists you switch on (for example from GitHub or the list maintainers' own sites). The request reveals only the list address and normal connection details, such as your IP address, to that host. Lists can refresh in the background about twice a day when iOS allows it.
  • Diagnostic services, only when you tap a check: api.ipify.org and ident.me (to show your public IP address), DNS provider test pages, and, for the in-app Adblock Test, a list of well-known advertising and tracking domains used to measure what is blocked.

Safari

The Safari content blockers apply rules inside Safari. Safari does not tell VayuSetu which pages you visit or what was blocked, and VayuSetu does not read your browsing.

What VayuSetu does not do

  • It does not collect, store or transmit the websites you visit or the content of your traffic.
  • It does not use third-party analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • It does not sell or share personal data, and it does not build profiles about you.
  • It does not require an account or any personal information.

Permissions

  • VPN configuration — required by iOS and macOS to add and start a VPN or local DNS tunnel.
  • Notifications (optional) — to tell you when a connection changes.
  • Camera (optional) — only to scan a WireGuard QR code; images are not stored or uploaded.
  • Live Activity (optional) — shows the tunnel state on the Lock Screen.

Children

VayuSetu is a general-purpose network utility and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
